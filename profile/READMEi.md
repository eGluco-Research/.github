About the Project
eGluco Research is the official GitHub organization for the research and development repositories of the eGluco project.
The project brings together software solutions focused on digital health, biomedical engineering, patient monitoring, data analysis, and support for scientific research.
This organization preserves different stages of the eGluco development, including legacy versions, desktop applications, and the current mobile applications for patients and researchers.
